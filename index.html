CROSSLE

key top = acrosskey bottom = down

How to play

Guess two 5-letter words at once: one across and one down. They share a single letter where they cross — and the crossing point moves every round.

Fill all 9 squares and press Enter. Each word is scored on its own, Wordle-style: green = right spot, yellow = in that word elsewhere, gray = not in that word.

The shared square can split: the top-left half is its score in the across word, the bottom-right half is its score in the down word.

Keyboard keys split the same way: top half for across, bottom half for down.

Solve a word and it locks in green. You get 6 tries to solve both. 20 rounds in all.
